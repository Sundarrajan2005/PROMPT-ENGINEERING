# Algorithm

The workflow for building and training a Large Language Model (LLM) can be described as follows:

1.Data Collection – Gather large-scale text data from books, websites, and research articles.
2.Data Preprocessing – Clean, normalize, and tokenize text into smaller units. 
3.Model Initialization – Set up the transformer-based architecture with self-attention and feedforward layers. 
4.Training Objective – Use self-supervised learning (next-token prediction) with cross-entropy loss. 
5.Optimization – Train with gradient descent using optimizers like AdamW, with learning rate scheduling. 
6.Fine-Tuning – Adapt the pretrained model for specific tasks such as summarization, question answering, or translation. 
7.Evaluation – Test the model on benchmark datasets and human evaluations.
8.Deployment – Deploy via APIs or integrate into applications like chatbots, search engines, and assistants.

# Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has rapidly advanced in the past decade, with Generative AI becoming one of the most influential fields. Unlike traditional AI systems that are designed for classification or prediction, Generative AI focuses on creating new content such as text, images, audio, or video. A key breakthrough in this area has been the development of transformer-based architectures and Large Language Models (LLMs).
Foundational Concepts of Generative AI
- Generative vs. Discriminative Models: Generative models create new data, while discriminative models classify existing data. - Training Paradigms: Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) and Reinforcement Learning. - Model Types: GANs, VAEs, Transformers.
Generative AI Architectures (Transformers)
- Attention Mechanism – Allows the model to focus on relevant words. - Self-Attention – Captures relationships between words in the same sequence. - Scalability – Supports large-scale training. Pretraining & Fine-Tuning – Enables transfer learning for various applications.
Applications of Generative AI
- NLP: ChatGPT, translation, summarization. - Computer Vision: DALL·E, MidJourney for image generation. - Healthcare: Drug discovery, medical imaging. - Entertainment: Music, gaming. Business: Chatbots, automated reports.
Impact of Scaling in LLMs
- Emergent Abilities – Reasoning, few-shot learning. - Performance Gains – Improved coherence and contextual accuracy. - Challenges – High computational cost, environmental concerns, risk of misinformation.
Large Language Models (LLMs) and How They Are Built
Steps: Data Collection → Preprocessing → Transformer Architecture → Training (self-supervised) → Fine-tuning → Evaluation → Deployment.
